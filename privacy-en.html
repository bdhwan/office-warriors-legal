Privacy Policy — Office Warriors

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Publisher: Altamirasoft (알타미라소프트) · Contact: bdhwan@gmail.com

1. Data we collect

None. Office Warriors (회사원 대전쟁) has no accounts or logins and contains no advertising, analytics, payment or crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not send any information to us or to third parties.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. We cannot access them.

3. Notifications

On Android, if you allow it, the app schedules at most one local reminder per day on your device. These are not server push notifications and can be turned off in Settings. The iOS version sends no notifications.

4. Permissions

  • Internet (Android): engine default; not used to transmit data.
  • Vibrate (Android): haptic feedback.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): optional local reminders.

5. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Contact emails

Tapping "Contact" opens your mail app. Emails you choose to send are used only to reply to you and are deleted after the inquiry is handled.

7. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
