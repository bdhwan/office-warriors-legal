Privacy Policy — Office Warriors

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Publisher: Altamirasoft (알타미라소프트) · Contact: bdhwan@gmail.com

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. Office Warriors has no accounts, logins or purchases, and we do not send your information to our own servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad after a battle, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app launch information, device information (model, OS version, language) and diagnostic/performance data. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We only see aggregated AdMob reports.

3. Your choices

On iOS the app asks for tracking permission on first launch; if you decline, the IDFA is not used, and you can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's ad settings. Where consent is required (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), consent is collected with Google's User Messaging Platform and can be changed or withdrawn any time from "Privacy options" in the game's Settings. If you decline personalized ads you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests. Every game feature works the same whatever you choose.

4. Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings and ad-frequency counters (time of the last full-screen ad, battles since then) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Notifications

On Android, if you allow it, the app schedules at most one local reminder per day on your device. These are not server push notifications and can be turned off in Settings. The iOS version does not send notifications.

6. Permissions

Internet / network state (Android, to load ads), Advertising ID (Android, for ads), Vibrate (haptics), Notifications (Android 13+, optional local reminders), Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency, optional, for personalized ads).

7. Retention

We do not retain advertising data. Google's retention follows its privacy policy. Support emails are used only to reply and are deleted after the inquiry is handled.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is limited to the T (teen) rating or below. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights and contact

For access or deletion requests or any question, email bdhwan@gmail.com. You can manage data processed by Google at https://myaccount.google.com or through the device settings above.

10. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
